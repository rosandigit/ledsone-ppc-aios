## 1. Purpose

**This record applies the Production Principle to the seven Phase 1b skills, and
does nothing else.**

**The architectural question it answers:**

> For each Phase 1b skill, has the Owner made the production determination that
> `decisions/DECISION_PRODUCTION_PRINCIPLE.md` requires — and if so, what is it?

**Why this record exists.** `decisions/DECISION_PRODUCTION_PRINCIPLE.md`
establishes that production of a Published Review Output requires an explicit
Owner governance determination, and that no such determination existed for
anything at the time it was recorded. It also left open **what a valid
determination consists of** and **whether determinations are made per skill, per
skill type, or collectively**. Until those two questions were answered, no
production determination could be made in a form the repository could rely on.

**This record is the place where per-skill production determinations are
recorded.** The Owner has now answered the two governance questions above. **The
per-skill determinations themselves have not been supplied**, and this record
records that fact rather than supplying them.

**This record applies existing governance. It defines none.** The Production
Principle is not restated, not extended and not reinterpreted here; it is
referenced by path only. See **5**.
